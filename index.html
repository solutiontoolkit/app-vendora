Vendora

Smart Shop & Sales Management Application

What is Vendora?

Vendora is a powerful shop management application designed to help business owners track sales, profits, stock, and cashiers in real time. Data is securely stored in the cloud, so users can log in from any device without losing records.

Key Features

  • Real-time sales and stock tracking
  • Profit calculation per day, week, and month
  • Auto-generated receipts (PDF & Email)
  • Multiple cashier accounts (up to 20 on paid plan)
  • Shared cloud database
  • Admin & cashier notes (internal chat)
  • Quick snap camera for transaction security
  • Android Tablet & Desktop support
